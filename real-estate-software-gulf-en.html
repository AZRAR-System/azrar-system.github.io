Real Estate Software for Saudi Arabia & the Gulf

For offices and property management firms in Riyadh, Jeddah, Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Kuwait City, Doha, Muscat and Manama: Gulf currencies, a bilingual interface and local control of your data.

October 20, 2026 · 4 min read

What sets Gulf real estate offices apart?

Contracts in different currencies, teams where some read Arabic and some English, many owners, and larger firms needing several devices and users. AZRAR is built around exactly these needs.

What AZRAR offers

Gulf currencies

Saudi riyal (SAR), UAE dirham (AED), Kuwaiti dinar (KWD), Qatari riyal (QAR), Omani rial (OMR) and Bahraini dinar (BHD), plus the Jordanian dinar and US dollar.

A bilingual interface

Arabic (right-to-left) and English (left-to-right) with an instant switch, so the whole team works in its own language.

Configurable VAT on invoices

The VAT engine supports both cases: tax added on top of the amount, or an amount that already includes tax, at a rate you set.

For larger firms

The Enterprise plan supports unlimited devices and SQL Server for large offices, with team training and dedicated support.

Local data, works offline

A local database on your machine, so there is no outside server holding your clients' and contracts' data.

Straight talk: AZRAR does not currently integrate with Saudi Arabia's Ejar platform or with government e-invoicing systems. If your authority requires contract registration or invoicing through an official platform, keep doing that there; AZRAR runs your office's internal work: contracts, installments, collection, commissions, owner statements and maintenance.

Common questions

Does it support more than one currency?

Yes, every transaction is recorded in its own currency, which suits offices managing properties in more than one country.

Is there a free trial?

Yes, 30 days with every feature and no credit card. Prices are on the pricing section.
