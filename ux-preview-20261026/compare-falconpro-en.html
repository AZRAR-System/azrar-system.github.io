AZRAR vs FalconPro

An objective comparison to help you pick the right real estate system for your office — no exaggeration, sources included.

AZRAR and FalconPro are both real estate office management systems serving the Arab market, but built on very different philosophies: AZRAR is built entirely around fully local, offline-first operation, while FalconPro is built primarily as a cloud platform with a separate Windows version as an option. The table below compares the core differences.

Which one fits your office?

Choose AZRAR if:

  • You want your office's data to stay fully local with no dependency on an external cloud
  • You want clear, published pricing from day one instead of requesting a quote
  • Your office is focused on real estate and doesn't need a bundled general accounting/HR system
  • You want to trial the full product for 30 days before deciding

Choose FalconPro if:

  • You want to access your data from any device via the cloud
  • You need a broader scope covering general accounting, HR/payroll, and CRM in one system
  • You run a company with multiple branches/entities that needs centralized cloud management
