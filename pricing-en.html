Buying from the Gulf and other Arab countries

Try AZRAR free for 30 days before buying a licence. Customers outside Jordan can pay by international bank transfer received in Jordanian dinars at Bank al Etihad. CliQ is also available within Jordan. Card payments are currently unavailable.

Amount and transfer fees

Base plan prices are in US dollars. The purchase review step shows the final JOD amount and the adopted exchange rate. The sender covers sending and intermediary bank fees so the full required amount arrives in JOD. The receiving account does not accept US dollars. Check availability and fees with your bank before paying.

Transfer confirmation and licence delivery

Bank details appear after reviewing the plan, selecting bank transfer and confirming the details. Email your order reference and transfer evidence to the support address shown in the payment step. Incoming funds are checked manually; the licence is delivered as soon as payment is confirmed. A transfer submission receipt alone does not confirm receipt of funds.

Currencies supported in the application are separate from the licence payment currency. Configurable tax settings do not establish integration with government e-invoicing or tenancy registration platforms. Check your local requirements before relying on the software for these purposes.
